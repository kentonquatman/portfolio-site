Placeholder story: nobody enjoys checkout; the best outcome is that it disappears. The original flow treated every field as equally important, so shoppers hit a wall of forms before they had any reason to trust the purchase. Reordering the steps — and designing the moments between them, so the flow always answers "where am I and what happens next" — is what this project is really about.
