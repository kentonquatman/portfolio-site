Placeholder story: banking apps tend to show people everything at once — every account, every product, every offer — so the one number people open the app for gets buried. This project started by asking what someone actually needs in the first five seconds, and the answer became the design: one balance that means something, and the three tasks people do every week within a thumb's reach.
